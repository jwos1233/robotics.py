Who supplies US roller screws

Share of tracked origins by import value · HTS 8483.40.8000
These origins are not one market
Since the 2024-11 break, Japan ships $218/unit and Switzerland $15/unit — a 15× gap, on 1.35m Swiss units against 182k Japanese. Whatever moves under this code from Switzerland is not the same product as the Japanese precision stream. So Switzerland gaining share does not mean it is displacing Japan in roller screws; two separate streams sit inside one tariff code and move independently. The same mix problem that disqualified the price read applies to share. Treat the shift as a question to investigate, not a conclusion.

Why the price read was abandoned

US imports from Japan — reported unit count, monthly
Reported quantity breaks at 2024-11
Imports from Japan held value steady — $3.56m in October, $2.15m in November — while the reported unit count fell from 66,421 to 4,796. No supply chain loses 93% of unit volume at flat value, so this is a change in what filers report, not an economic event. Quantity and unit value are not comparable across this boundary. Value is the only leg that survives.
